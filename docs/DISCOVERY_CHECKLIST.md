# Discovery Checklist

Use this checklist before starting development.

## Management Meeting

- Confirm the main business problem.
- Confirm that the first system should be workforce management, not AI CCTV.
- Confirm who will approve the project.
- Confirm who will test the first version.
- Confirm which department or area should be used for the first pilot.

## Employee Data

- Collect sample employee data fields.
- Decide required fields.
- Decide optional fields.
- Decide who can edit employee data.
- Decide how employee IDs should work.

## Shifts

- Confirm morning shift time.
- Confirm night shift time.
- Confirm rules for shift swap.
- Confirm whether both employees must agree.
- Confirm whether manager approval is always required.

## Leave And Rest Requests

- Confirm leave balance rules.
- Confirm holiday scheduling rules.
- Confirm rest hour rules.
- Confirm conflict rules.
- Confirm approval rules.

## Roles And Permissions

- Employee
- Manager or supervisor
- HR admin
- System admin

For each role, decide:

- what they can see
- what they can create
- what they can approve
- what they can edit
- what reports they can export

## Reports

- Daily attendance or staffing report
- Employees on leave
- Pending approvals
- Shift swap history
- Leave balance report
- Department staffing report

## Privacy And Trust

- Explain that the first system does not require biometrics.
- Explain that the system starts by helping employees and managers.
- Explain that AI CCTV is a later integration after the foundation is ready.
- Decide who can view sensitive employee data.
- Decide audit log requirements.

## Pilot Plan

- Choose one department or production line.
- Add a limited number of employees first.
- Test requests and approvals.
- Collect manager feedback.
- Collect employee feedback.
- Improve before expanding to the whole factory.
