# Management Meeting Agenda

## Meeting Goal

Agree that the project should begin with a factory workforce management system, then expand later into attendance and AI CCTV.

## Suggested Opening

The original idea was to build an AI CCTV system. During planning, we found that the AI camera needs reliable operational data first. It needs to know who is working, which shift they are assigned to, whether shift changes are approved, and who is responsible for each zone.

Because of that, the better first step is to build a workforce management portal. This will help employees and managers immediately, and it will also prepare the factory for future AI integration.

## Discussion Points

### 1. Current Problems

- Employee requests are handled by paper or verbal communication.
- Shift changes may not be clearly recorded.
- Leave planning depends on manual checking.
- Managers may not have a fast daily view of staffing.
- AI CCTV cannot make fair decisions without approved shift and zone data.

### 2. Proposed First System

- Employee database
- Morning and night shift records
- Leave and holiday requests
- Rest hour requests
- Shift swap requests
- Manager approvals
- Basic reports
- Mobile-friendly employee portal

### 3. Why This Helps The AI CCTV Project

- The AI system will know who is expected to work.
- Approved shift swaps will be visible to the system.
- Zone assignments can be connected to schedules.
- Future alerts will be more accurate.
- Employees will see the project as useful before it becomes monitoring.

### 4. Pilot Suggestion

Start with one department, production line, or small employee group.

Pilot goals:

- test employee request flow
- test manager approvals
- confirm shift rules
- collect feedback
- improve before full rollout

### 5. Decisions Needed From Management

- Should we begin with workforce management before AI CCTV?
- Which department should be used for the pilot?
- Who will be the manager approver during testing?
- What employee data can be used?
- What are the exact shift times?
- What leave and rest rules should the system follow?

## Proposed Next Step

Approve Phase 1 planning and discovery. After Phase 1, we will present a clear first-version scope and start building Phase 2: the core workforce database.
